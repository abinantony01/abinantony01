About

An independent R&D practice inside a hardware company.

I’m Abin Antony, R&D Engineer at Vi Microsystems Pvt Ltd, Chennai. Days are spent on schematic, layout, bring-up — turning an idea into a board that actually runs.

The work sits between silicon and software: 2-layer PCBs, STM32 and RP2350 firmware, Android over BLE, ROS robots, and TinyML at the edge. Clarity first. Craft always.

Practice
