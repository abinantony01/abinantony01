profile

R&D engineer. Hardware first. Analog when it matters.

I’m Abin Antony, an R&D engineer at Vi Microsystems, Chennai. I design boards, bring them up, and stay with the analog until the numbers are real — not just until the LED turns on.

Current desk: Single Pair Ethernet (10BASE-T1S / 10BASE-T1L), analog front ends for instruments, and Lattice iCE40 FPGA work on the weekends. Firmware that other people can install — ViSPE ADIN1110 is in the Arduino Library Manager.

From Idukki, Kerala. Based in Chennai. Schematic → layout → firmware → the bit that fails on the bench.
