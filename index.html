who even is this

A rookie with a hot iron and too many unfinished boards.

Hey — I’m Abin. I work as an R&D engineer at Vi Microsystems, Chennai, which still feels a bit unreal. I like electronics. Like, actually like them. Schematics at 1am, JLCPCB tracking emails, that first LED.

I’m not a studio. I’m a hobbyist who got a job doing the hobby. 2-layer boards, STM32, Raspberry Pi, Android apps that talk to my own hardware, robots that currently refuse to drive straight. TinyML because running a model on a chip is just… cool.

From Idukki, Kerala. Learning in public. Please roast my layouts.
